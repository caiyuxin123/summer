# Notice

This package contains research code, manuscript files, and the frozen RTS-GMLC release-0.2 raw-source subset required to reproduce the reported experiments offline.

Bundled RTS-GMLC source data remain subject to the upstream DOE/NREL/ALLIANCE data-use terms. The source notice and attribution information are preserved in:

`data/raw/RTS_GMLC_DATA_USE_DISCLAIMER.md`

File-level provenance, included scope, upstream blob identifiers, and SHA256 checksums are recorded in:

`data/raw/raw_data_manifest.csv`

The bundled load and wind time-series files are purpose-limited source subsets containing only the official rows required by this paper; they are not presented as full annual RTS-GMLC archives.
